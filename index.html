01 — About Me

Building Ideas Into Reality

Aboobakker Fahiz is the Founder and Chairman of TBD GROUP PVT LTD COMPANY, a growing infrastructure and development startup based in Mangaluru, Karnataka. He leads the group's multi-wing operations across construction, premium real estate and luxury interior solutions. Alongside his business journey, Fahiz has been involved in youth and student leadership in Dakshina Kannada, previously serving as Vice President of the All College Students Association (ACSA). His professional vision is built around one principle: “Three Visions, One Mission.”

03 — My Journey

From Leadership
to Entrepreneurship

Student Leadership

Before entering corporate management, Fahiz was active among the student community in Dakshina Kannada. His experience as Vice President of the All College Students Association helped develop public speaking, organization, crowd coordination and leadership skills.

The Three Visions Concept

Fahiz transitioned from student advocacy into entrepreneurship, creating a business ecosystem capable of connecting land acquisition, construction and modern interior development.

TBD GROUP

This vision developed into TBD GROUP PVT LTD COMPANY, with three specialized business wings: SR PRIME Properties, TBD Builders & Developers and AURELIV Interiors & Design.

Current Vision

Today, Fahiz leads the group with a focus on building a modern, trusted and technology-forward infrastructure business in coastal Karnataka.
